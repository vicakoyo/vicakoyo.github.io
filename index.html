Selected Work

Operational problems turned into measurable outcomes.

Short case studies showing the type of problems I have worked on, the decisions involved and the resulting operational impact.

Supply Chain • Cost Optimisation

Restructuring logistics costs

Reviewed warehousing, storage and final-mile cost structures, benchmarked alternatives, negotiated commercial terms and supported a transition to a more efficient logistics model.

Result: approximately £400K annual warehouse/3PL savings plus approximately £120K in final-mile savings.

Quality • Reverse Logistics

Reducing product returns

Used return-cause analysis alongside supplier quality, packaging, handling and delivery reviews to address recurring failure modes across the product journey.

Result: returns reduced from approximately 12% to 4% by CBM.

Logistics • Operational Recovery

Protecting a time-critical container movement

Responded to a capacity constraint affecting approximately ten inbound containers by sourcing an alternative operating route, coordinating warehouse capacity and accelerating movement within available free time.

Result: all affected containers moved within free time, avoiding demurrage exposure and further operational delay.

Business Operations • Performance Management

Building leadership visibility

Designed a 19-KPI leadership scorecard spanning customer, workforce, recruitment and commercial performance, integrated into structured leadership and quarterly review rhythms.

Result: clearer operating visibility, ownership and management discussion around performance and priorities.
